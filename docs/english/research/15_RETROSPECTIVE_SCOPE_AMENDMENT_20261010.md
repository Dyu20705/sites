## 1. Decision and estimand — PROPOSED

Amend M1 to a reproducible descriptive demonstration of **first-version title lexical prevalence within the observed, frozen 2026 arXiv cs.SE query**, grouped retrospectively by January–June 2019 UTC v1 submission month.

Proposed primary question: *Can a researcher inspect and reproduce the number and proportion of distinct observed works whose v1 title matches the versioned fuzzing rule, understand denominator and selection effects, and trace every contribution to frozen source evidence?*

The estimand is a finite-corpus descriptive quantity, not an estimate of complete historical cs.SE activity. Report n, N, n/N and evidence/uncertainty. Do not infer historical public availability, emergence, growth/decline of a scientific field, importance, adoption, causality, topic recall, or validated trend detection. A zero lexical count does not mean no fuzzing research. Semantic relevance is a different construct and remains independently unmeasured. No scientific novelty claim is made.

## 4. D08-R — lexical descriptive indicator proposal

Rule `RE-20260927-01-documented-word-boundary-v1`, case-insensitive `\bfuzz(?:ing|er|ers)?\b` on v1 titles. For eligible distinct works C_b: `N(b)=|C_b|`, `n(b)=Σ match(w)`, `p(b)=n(b)/N(b)` when defined. Propose share as the displayed primary indicator with **n and N mandatory**. Count alone is the serious simpler alternative; it hides denominator differences, while share remains vulnerable to selection/alias drift. Neither supports a scientific growth label.

Measured n **0/1/2/1/4/1**, total **9/598 = 1.5050%**, support only 0–4/month. May's four matches are not evidence of scientific growth. All values, raw refs, sensitivities, controls, prior art and correction history remain in [report 14](14_D08_DEVELOPMENT_EVALUATION_20261010.md); this packet changes no rule or measurement.

Support policy: publish n≥0 when all relevant fields are known; share requires N≥1 and valid event/title fields. Missing/invalid title stays in N, makes n/p null, and permits explicitly bounded known-match shares. Missing/invalid event makes monthly denominators unresolved; empty N gives undefined share. Conflicting v1 rows fail closed; revisions do not contribute again. These are definability criteria, not thresholds proving adequate statistical support. **Directional trend labels are disabled for every support value**; no finite n threshold is proposed as a trend-validity guarantee.

Sensitivity set stays frozen: exact fuzzing, current title, v1 category subset, exclusion of nine late IDs solely as perturbation, adjacent two-month ratio of sums and decoded-pattern equality diagnostic. Report every result, including the five-match exact-fuzzing variant. Never choose a different concept/window to make the graph attractive. Reverse the signal recommendation if reviewer interpretation fails, denominator is invalid or semantic checks later reveal misleading use; propose changes prospectively without rewriting this development evidence.
